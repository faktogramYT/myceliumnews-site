What we publish

Space and science

Missions, observations and research results explained in plain English, with the key number up front.

Technology and AI

What was announced, what it can actually do and what is still unknown.

Nature and the world

Animals, ecosystems and events that deserve a closer look, told without sensationalism.

Videos are about 30 seconds long, vertical, captioned word by word, and published on TikTok and Instagram.

How we work

Real pictures first

We build videos from authentic footage and photographs, such as public-domain material from space agencies, and use illustrations only when they clearly explain something that cannot be filmed.

Facts we can source

A claim goes into a video only if we confirmed it in a primary source or in two independent sources. We avoid rumours and unconfirmed accusations, and we word uncertain points as uncertain.

Transparent about AI

The narration in our videos uses a synthetic (AI) voice. Pictures are real, captions follow the spoken words, and we do not use AI to fabricate footage of real events.

Corrections

If we get something wrong we fix it and say so. Report an error through the contact page.

MyceliumNEWS Desktop

MyceliumNEWS Desktop is a small Windows application that connects to a TikTok account through TikTok Login Kit, after that account holder approves access, and shows the account's public statistics: follower, like and video counts, and the view, like, comment and share counts of its public videos. It is read-only. See exactly what it does and what data it uses.
